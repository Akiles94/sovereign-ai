# Sovereign AI

Private AI infrastructure. Community-owned. Open source. Your data, your control.

---

## The Problem

Every time you use ChatGPT or Claude:
- Your code goes to their servers
- Your ideas are logged
- Your company data is retained
- They can use it for training
- You have no real guarantee it won't be exploited

**This is not privacy. This is convenience at the cost of sovereignty.**

---

## The Solution

A small datacenter running open-weight models (Llama, Qwen). Funded and owned by the community that uses it.

- **Private:** Data never leaves our infrastructure
- **Open source:** Code is auditable, you know what happens
- **Affordable:** Significantly cheaper than OpenAI/Anthropic per token
- **Yours:** Community-owned, not venture capital owned

Security and privacy are engineered, not promised: TLS 1.3, no prompt/response logging, VRAM-only processing, 90-day log retention. Full breakdown: [`docs/security.md`](docs/security.md)

---

## How It Works

You pay per use (€1-2 per million tokens) via a simple HTTP API — same integration pattern you're used to with any LLM provider, just pointed at infrastructure the community owns.

Revenue covers operational costs. Any surplus is reinvested in expanding capacity, decided transparently by the community.

## 12-Month Roadmap

**Months 1-3: Foundation**
- Legal registration (nonprofit association)
- Hardware procurement and setup (GPUs, networking)
- Core platform development (orchestrator, API, client)
- Internal testing

**Months 4-6: Closed Beta**
- 10-20 trusted early users
- Bug fixes, reliability improvements
- Governance structure defined
- Real-world feedback loop

**Months 7-9: Gradual Expansion**
- Open to wider community (50-100 users)
- Public documentation, onboarding flow
- Pricing refinement based on real usage
- First real usage data at scale

**Months 10-12: Evaluation**
- Full year of operational data
- Revenue analysis (are we sustainable?)
- Community governance vote: scale, pivot, or wind down
- Plan for Year 2 (second funding round if applicable)

*This is a side project alongside full-time work. Progress is realistic, not rushed. Correctness matters more than speed.*

**For you (the user):**
- No central corporation holding your data

## Legal & Governance

Sovereign AI is set up as a **nonprofit association in Spain**, not a company — no shares, no profit distribution, annual external audit required by law. The community governs itself through an annual General Assembly and a quarterly Community Council; the founder (Oscar Vinueza) keeps a protected minimum vote share and a veto limited to existential threats to the mission (selling to VCs, converting to for-profit) — a defensive power that cannot be used to change the project's core principles unilaterally.
